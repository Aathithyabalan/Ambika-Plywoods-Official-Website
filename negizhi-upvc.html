Who We Are

Quality uPVC Frames,
Built for Dindigul's Climate.

Negizhi UPVC is the dedicated windows and doors division of Ambikaa Plywoods. We bring the same commitment to quality that Ambikaa has been known for over 15 years, now focused entirely on uPVC fabrication.

Our uPVC profiles are sourced from certified manufacturers and fabricated in-house by skilled craftsmen — ensuring every frame fits precisely and performs reliably for decades.

Our Products & Services

Everything you need for premium uPVC windows and doors — designed, fabricated and installed by us.

Sliding Windows
