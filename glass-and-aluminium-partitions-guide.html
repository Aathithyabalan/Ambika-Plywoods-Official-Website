Glass and Aluminium Partitions: Types and Buying Guide

14 Oct 20265 min readBy Ambika Plywoods, Dindigul

Partitions divide space without building walls. They are faster to install, easier to change later and let in light. Whether you are separating a bedroom, creating office cabins or splitting a shop floor, this guide covers the main types and what to check.

Common partition types

  • Aluminium-framed glass: toughened glass in a slim aluminium frame. Affordable, strong and the most popular for offices and shops.
  • Frameless glass: large toughened panels fixed with minimal fittings. Looks premium and maximises light, but costs more and needs precise installation.
  • Solid aluminium and composite panels: for areas where privacy matters, such as storerooms, wet areas and cabin walls.
  • Half-glass, half-panel: solid at the bottom, glass at the top, a good balance of privacy and light.

Privacy options

  • Frosted or acid-washed glass: blocks the view, lets in light.
  • Printed glass: patterns, gradients or your logo for branding and privacy.
  • Films and etching: a flexible way to add privacy bands at eye level.
  • Blinds between glass or on the surface: adjustable privacy.

What to check before you order

  • Use toughened glass for safety, ideally 8 to 12 mm depending on panel size.
  • Check the aluminium section thickness and finish (anodised or powder-coated).
  • Plan door positions, locks and closers early so the glass can be cut and toughened correctly.
  • Think about sound. Single glass does not block noise well, so ask about thicker or double-layer options for meeting rooms.
  • Level and fix the floor and ceiling lines properly, because poor leveling shows up in the final gaps.

Home uses

  • Separating the living and dining area while keeping light
  • Kitchen partitions that contain cooking smells but keep the space bright
  • Pooja room dividers with printed or carved glass
  • Study corners and work-from-home cabins

Office and shop uses

  • Manager cabins and meeting rooms
  • Reception and waiting areas
  • Showroom dividers and product display zones

Frequently Asked Questions

Are glass partitions soundproof?
Single glass blocks little noise. Thicker glass, laminated glass or double-layer systems reduce sound, so ask about them for meeting rooms.
What glass thickness is used for partitions?
Usually toughened glass of 8 to 12 mm depending on the panel size and whether it is framed or frameless.
Can I add privacy to a clear glass partition?
Yes. Frosted or acid-washed glass, UV printing, etching, films or blinds can all add privacy while still letting light through.
Can a partition be changed or removed later?
Aluminium-framed partitions can be modified, moved or removed far more easily than a brick wall, which is a big reason people choose them.
