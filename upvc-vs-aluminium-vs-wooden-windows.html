UPVC vs Aluminium vs Wooden Windows: Which Is Best?

14 Oct 20266 min readBy Ambika Plywoods, Dindigul

Windows and doors are a long-term decision. In Tamil Nadu you need frames that handle intense heat, monsoon rain, humidity and termites without needing repainting every other year. Here is how the three common options compare.

UPVC windows

UPVC (unplasticised polyvinyl chloride) frames are reinforced with steel and fitted with sealed glazing.

  • Pros: termite-proof and rot-proof, low maintenance, good sound and dust insulation, no repainting, good rain sealing.
  • Cons: higher upfront cost than basic aluminium, limited colour range compared with painted wood, and quality varies widely between profiles.
  • Best for: homes near roads, apartments, coastal and humid areas, and anyone who wants low maintenance.

Aluminium windows

  • Pros: slim, strong frames, wide range of finishes, good for large sliding panels, cost-effective.
  • Cons: metal conducts heat, so basic frames do not insulate as well as UPVC, and cheaper sections can look less refined.
  • Best for: shopfronts, large openings, sliding doors and budget projects.

Wooden windows

  • Pros: classic look, can be carved and polished, easy to repair.
  • Cons: prone to termites, warping and swelling in humidity, needs regular polishing, and good timber is expensive.
  • Best for: heritage-style homes where appearance matters most and maintenance is accepted.

Side-by-side summary

  • Termite resistance: UPVC and aluminium are immune. Wood is vulnerable.
  • Maintenance: UPVC is lowest. Wood is highest.
  • Sound insulation: UPVC with double glazing is typically best.
  • Appearance: wood is warmest, aluminium is slimmest, UPVC is clean and modern.

What to check before you buy

  • Ask about the profile wall thickness and the steel reinforcement inside UPVC frames.
  • Check hardware quality: hinges, locks and multi-point locking.
  • Ask about glass type: single, double glazed or toughened.
  • Make sure fabrication is done from measured sizes, not stock sizes.
  • Look at the warranty and who provides service after installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are UPVC windows good for hot weather?
UPVC frames do not conduct heat the way bare metal does, and sealed glazing reduces heat entering the room. Quality varies by profile, so ask about wall thickness and glass options.
Do UPVC windows need painting or polishing?
No. The colour is part of the material, so cleaning with a damp cloth is usually all they need.
Can termites damage UPVC windows?
No. UPVC is not a food source for termites, unlike wooden frames.
How long do UPVC windows last?
Good profiles with proper steel reinforcement and quality hardware commonly last for decades with little upkeep. Hardware quality and installation matter as much as the frame.
